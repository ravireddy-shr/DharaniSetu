1. Executive Summary & Problem Solved

In traditional Indian land governance, citizens requiring land-related services (such as Title Mutation, Land Conversion (NALA), Building Permission NOCs, or Boundary Verification) must apply separately across multiple uncoordinated government departments: Revenue Department, Survey & Settlement, Registration & Stamps, and Town Planning / Municipalities.

DharaniSetu bridges this divide by delivering a unified Digital Public Infrastructure platform featuring a Single Case File System, Dynamic Departmental Workflow Routing, Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), 360° Cadastral GIS Parcel Intelligence, and In-Browser Document Scrutiny.

📁 Single Case File Paradigm

Each citizen submission is assigned a single, immutable Application ID (e.g. DS-AP-2026-000450) that moves sequentially through each required departmental desk.

🛰️ Cadastral GIS & AI Scrutiny

Seamlessly integrates satellite boundaries, PostGIS geo-polygons, and AI OCR to cross-match Sale Deed declared extent with actual cadastral coordinates.

3. Dynamic Department Routing Matrix

Service Desk Sequence Required Statutory Verifications
Mutation / Ownership Update 1. Revenue Officer (VRO) → 2. Survey Officer → 3. Tahsildar Title deed validity, Pattadar passbook, physical pegging, final gazette endorsement.
Building Permission NOC 1. Town Planning → 2. Survey Officer → 3. Tahsildar Zoning regulation, master plan alignment, road setback demarcation, safety NOC.
Land Conversion (NALA) 1. Revenue Officer (VRO) → 2. Town Planning → 3. Tahsildar Agricultural cess clearance, non-agricultural land assessment, statutory conversion order.
Boundary Demarcation / Survey 1. Survey Officer → 2. Tahsildar Differential GPS field measurement, adjacent neighbor notice, boundary rectification.
Land Grievance & Disputes 1. Revenue Officer (VRO) → 2. Survey Officer → 3. Tahsildar Contested petition hearing, encroachment probe, Tahsildar certified clearance order.

4. Key Technological Innovations

🔍 Document Inspection Protocol

Replaced unscrutinized button clicks with a full-screen inspection modal featuring zoom (+/-), 90° rotation, legibility checklists, and decisive "Verify & Accept" or "Reject with Statutory Reason" controls.

🤖 AI Area Discrepancy Analyzer

Compares Sale Deed OCR extracted extent (e.g. 1.00 Acre) against the satellite cadastral survey polygon (0.90 Acre), alerting the Tahsildar to issue reconciliation directives or order DGPS resurvey.

5. Live Demonstration Sequence for SIH Jury

  1. Start Platform: npm run dev (Platform live at http://localhost:5173).
  2. Citizen Portal: Login as Citizen → Explore GIS 360° parcel → Submit Land Grievance with uploaded deed → Obtain Token ID.
  3. Revenue Officer (VRO) Desk: Open application → Click "Open & Inspect" → Check deed legibility → Verify & Approve Revenue stage.
  4. Survey Officer Desk: Verify cadastral boundary & coordinates → Approve Survey stage.
  5. Tahsildar Desk: Review AI discrepancy & field reports → Issue final statutory approval.
  6. Citizen Clearance: Log back in as Citizen → Click "View Proof" → Download official Tahsildar Certified Land Ownership & Dispute Clearance Order with QR code.
